The SecurityConfig allows public access to all files under /api/uploads/**. Since post-event reports store sensitive photos and feedback files in predictable paths (e.g., reports/{eventID}/photos/{filename}), an attacker can easily guess these paths and download sensitive event data and PII without authentication.
